Play Solo spawns a character, which needs fonts/character.rbxm from a Roblox content folder. Content is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Controls

  • Right mouse drag to turn the camera, wheel to zoom.
  • Left click uses tools and the engine menu.
  • W A S D and the arrow keys move a character once Play Solo is running.
  • On a touch screen, drag to turn the camera and tap to click.

What this is

The OpenRBX engine (a 2007 Roblox reconstruction) built for the browser: physics, Lua and the renderer are the real code, running offline in a single thread. There is no networking, sound or multiplayer.

License and content

OpenRBX is licensed under the GNU GPL v3, not released into the public domain. Its source is at github.com/OpenRBX-org/OpenRBX. The original game art is not part of it, so this page uses flat placeholder textures plus the BSD-licensed font and sky from G3D. Bring your own content folder for the original look.
